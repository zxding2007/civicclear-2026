# CivicClear — Official-source cross-check
**Review date:** November 12, 2026 (ET)  
**Reviewer:** ChatGPT-assisted independent primary-source review; **NOT independent human/team sign-off**  
**Scope:** Four Toronto TMMIS decision histories, selected relevant passages in the linked City reports, enacted municipal property-tax by-laws, and the separate City of Toronto public consultation project site and mapping portal. The entire 37-page/30-page reports, their attachments, subsequent by-law enactment for planning, and every possible later item were not exhaustively assessed.

## Decision-by-decision evidence

### DM41.4 — Cummer Station

- [Official TMMIS agenda item](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.DM41.4): May 20–21, 2026 City Council, Ward **18 – Willowdale**. Status is **Received**; tracking says no further action will be taken on this item. A 23–0 roll-call vote was to **receive**, not to approve construction or fund the station.
- [Two-page councillor notice of motion](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/dm/bgrd/backgroundfile-286631.pdf): advocates reinstating Cummer Station and supplies arguments about Yonge North Subway Extension demand; it is a **proposal**, not an approval.
- **Audit result:** cached `received` label, date, ward, and the interpretation “not an approval” are supported. There is no evidenced current comment window.

### PH29.2 — Little Jamaica and Oakwood Vaughan

- [Official TMMIS item](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.PH29.2): Apr 14, 2026 **Planning and Housing Committee**, Wards **5, 8, 9 and 12**. Committee **adopted with amendments**, directed community consultation, and asked for a stakeholder advisory committee. This is not a final City Council approval of a completed framework.
- [March 27 proposals report](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/ph/bgrd/backgroundfile-285675.pdf): the framework is a **draft** and the staff recommendation is to consult, not a completed Council decision.
- **IMPORTANT UPDATE — separate from the agenda history:** The [City planning project page](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/planning-studies-initiatives/little-jamaica-oakwood-vaughan-planning-framework/) states the Stakeholder Advisory Committee has launched and the planned final report is now **Q1 2027**, whereas the original April motion requested **Q4 2026**.
- Its linked [Have Your Say interactive map](https://haveyoursay.toronto.ca/LJOV-Planning-Framework-CHRA) expressly advertises a public feedback window of **April 24–November 30, 2026**. This is the **specific map activity**, not proof that oral deputations or every other feedback channel is currently open. No time-of-day is given. The form may change and should be checked again before responding.
- **Audit result:** retained the `committee_only` status and added source-backed map deadline, direct participation link, end-date handling, and a note distinguishing original report-back request from newer project scheduling.

### PH29.1 — McCleary District temporary “Meanwhile Uses”

- [Official item](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.PH29.1): Ward **14 – Toronto–Danforth**. Committee adopted without amendment on **Apr 14**, and City Council adopted without amendments on **Apr 22–23, 2026**.
- [March 30 staff report](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/ph/bgrd/backgroundfile-285686.pdf): recommends allowing temporary non-residential uses, such as film, creative industry, sport, culture and retail, on five identified properties while future development proceeds. City Council's Part 4 requires joint provincial approvals *if required* relating to natural hazards before enactment of bills, subject to other stated conditions.
- **Audit result:** all three displayed stages are consistent with the record. The April 14 **statutory public meeting was held**; no verified new public-comment deadline is present on the item history. Do **not** equate the broader McCleary Precinct Plan (further work anticipated 2028/2029) with this item's zoning amendments.

### CC38.1 — 2026 budget / property taxes

- [Official item](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.CC38.1): City Council adopted with amendments **Feb 10, 2026**, **All** wards; budget implementation encompasses many subjects beyond the calculator.
- [2026 staff report](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/cc/bgrd/backgroundfile-284368.pdf): recommended budget and levy implementation; the report alone does not prove adoption. The official Council decision includes a specific amendment affecting the **small-business commercial tax subclass**; the fact that the *item* was amended should not be misread as proof that the residential calculation itself was amended.
- [Enacted 2026 Tax Levy By-law 133-2026](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/bylaws/2026/law0133.pdf): residential ending municipal rate **0.614311%**. [Enacted 2025 Tax Levy By-law 105-2025](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/bylaws/2025/law0105.pdf): residential ending municipal rate **0.601087%**.
- Both rates include municipal levies and City Building Fund, exclude education tax and assume the **same assessed value**. At $700,000, figures are $4,207.61 (2025), $4,300.18 (2026), and $92.57 illustrative difference. These are not personal property tax bills.
- **Audit result:** statuses, tax rates and illustrative arithmetic are correct; encourage use of the enacted by-law rather than relying only on staff-proposed rates.

## Source matching and limitations

1. Source quotations in existing curated item records were checked against the official City TMMIS pages. The new consultation quotations were cross-checked against the official City project site and its linked public feedback portal.
2. The four cached card records are **human-curated / source-linked demonstration data**, not evidence that Claude API extraction ran. Local `verify_card` checks whether excerpts occur in **bundled curated text**, not whether an external authority has independently certified each statement.
3. Staff reports and selected decision recommendations were read, but this **does not constitute an exhaustive legal review of every PDF page, attachment, statutory notice or subsequent enactment**. Cross-check original source pages before official municipal use.
4. We did not submit any public comments; we only read public pages. The mapping portal may collect visitor information if a resident submits feedback.
5. This audit was done by an AI assistant. The **independent human team approval remains pending** and must not be marked complete or misrepresented on the app.
