    A Toronto decision moves in 3 steps and the wording tells you which step you are reading.

    1

    Staff advise council

    City staff send a report saying what they advise. Nothing is decided at this step.

    The record says"Recommended"↓In plain words

    Advice only. No vote yet.

    2

    A committee of council votes

    Councillors on a committee debate it. This is where residents can ask to speak.

    The record says"Adopted with amendments"↓In plain words

    The committee agreed to a changed version. Not final.

    3

    City Council decides

    Council makes the final call. It can also just note an item without approving it.

    The record says"Received for information"↓In plain words

    Noted only. Not approved.

    Received for information is not approved. A committee vote is not City Council's final word. CivicClear tells you which one you are reading, and shows the line in the City's record that says so.

    From the City's record to a decision card

    Each of the four cards was drafted with an AI assistant (ChatGPT) from the saved City record. Three checks run before anything is shown.

    What the checks stop on a decision card

    • A made-up quote. If the words are not in the City's text, the line is marked unverified.
    • A wrong status. An unverified stage can never count as an approval.
    • A guessed deadline. A date shows only when an official page states it.

    Questions

    What people ask before they trust a page about their city.

    Read the code on GitHub

    Is this an official City of Toronto site?

    No. CivicClear is a student project for CIVIC Hack 2026. Every line links to the City's record, and the record wins if they differ.

    Does CivicClear call an AI model while I use it?

    No. The four cards were drafted ahead of time with ChatGPT from the City's text, then checked by code: every quote must appear in the saved record and the status is set by rule. A team member still has to sign each one off.

    Where does a council comment deadline come from?

    Only from an official City page. When the record names no deadline, CivicClear says so and links to the City's general Have your say page.

    Is the tax figure my real Toronto bill?

    No. It is the city portion at the same assessed value in both years. Education tax, reassessment, credits and fees are left out.

    Who might this page leave out?

    It is in English only, needs an internet connection and covers four decisions. A ward match means the ward is named in the record, not that your street is affected. If that leaves you out, call 311 or contact the City Clerk's office.

    How current are the decisions?

    Sources were checked on November 12, 2026. A consultation can close or be extended, so open the City page before you act.
